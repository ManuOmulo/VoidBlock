# Understanding Your Focus Insights

VoidBlock analytics don't just show numbers; they tell the story of your relationship with your device. Here is how to interpret each part of your report.

## 1. Focus Records (Weekly Bar Chart)

**The Story**: This shows your daily persistence.

- **What it is**: High bars indicate long periods of scheduled focus or manual blocking sessions.
- **Goal**: Look for consistency. A balanced bar chart (similar heights every day) is better for long-term habit building than one giant spike followed by empty days.

## 2. App Usage (Pie Chart)

**The Story**: This reveals your "time sinks."

- **What it is**: A breakdown of which apps you actually used during your day (including both allowed and blocked attempts).
- **Insight**: If a "Distraction" app like Social Media takes up >30% of the pie, it’s a signal to create a stricter schedule for that specific app.

## 3. Focus Trend (Bi-Weekly Trend)

**The Story**: This shows your long-term growth.

- **What it is**: A side-by-side comparison of your focus time over 14 days.
- **Goal**: You want to see the "valleys" slowly rising over time. It’s normal to have dips on weekends, but an upward slope indicates you are reclaiming your time.

## 4. Milestone Activity (Monthly Grid)

**The Story**: Your consistency map.

- **What it is**: A heatmap of your daily focus activity.
- **Insight**: Look for "Streaks." The darker the green, the more dedicated you were that day. A "broken" streak is just an opportunity to start a new, stronger one.

## 5. Productivity Score

**The Story**: The "Grade" of your focus.

- **Calculation**: We compare your `Blocked Attempts` vs. `Time Saved`.
- **Result**:
  - **80%+**: Elite Focus. You are mostly ignoring distractions.
  - **60-80%**: Good Progress. You are fighting the urge to open blocked apps.
  - **Below 60%**: High Friction. You are frequently trying to open blocked apps. Consider using **Hard Mode** to break the habit loop.

---

> [!TIP]
> Use these insights to adjust your **App Limits**. If an app is frequently appearing in your "Most Blocked" insight, try decreasing its daily limit by 5 minutes every week!
